Cybersecurity Policy

Security rules and requirements for ABC IT Solutions.

🔑 Password & Authentication

  • Use strong passwords.
  • Never share passwords.
  • Enable MFA for important accounts.
  • Do not reuse sensitive passwords.
  • Report compromised credentials immediately.

💻 Endpoint Security

  • Keep operating systems updated.
  • Enable endpoint protection.
  • Use host firewall protection.
  • Encrypt sensitive devices.
  • Lock devices when unattended.

📧 Email Security

  • Check suspicious emails carefully.
  • Do not open unexpected attachments.
  • Verify suspicious links.
  • Report phishing attempts.
  • Never send passwords by email.

📁 Data Protection

  • Access data according to job requirements.
  • Back up important information.
  • Protect confidential information.
  • Use encryption where appropriate.
  • Securely dispose of sensitive information.

B.Tech Viva Questions

Important questions and answers for project presentation.

What is cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, applications, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, and disruption.

What is Incident Response?

Incident Response is a structured process used to detect, investigate, contain, eradicate, and recover from security incidents.

What is BCP?

Business Continuity Planning focuses on keeping critical business operations running during disruptions.

What is DRP?

Disaster Recovery Planning focuses on restoring IT systems and data after a major disruption.

What is RTO?

Recovery Time Objective is the target time within which a service should be restored after a disruption.

What is RPO?

Recovery Point Objective defines the target amount of recent data that the organization can afford to lose.

Why use pfSense?

pfSense is used as a firewall to control and protect network traffic between external and internal networks.

Why use Wazuh?

Wazuh provides security monitoring and helps identify suspicious events and security-related activity.
