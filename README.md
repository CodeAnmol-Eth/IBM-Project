Cybersecurity Policy, Incident Response & Business Continuity Plan

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on developing a Cybersecurity Policy, Incident Response Plan, Business Continuity Plan, and Disaster Recovery Plan for a small business.

The project provides a structured framework to help a small business identify cybersecurity risks, protect critical information and systems, respond effectively to security incidents, and recover from unexpected disruptions.

🎯 Objectives

* Identify common cybersecurity risks and threats.
* Establish basic security policies and guidelines.
* Define procedures for responding to cybersecurity incidents.
* Protect critical business data and systems.
* Develop backup and disaster recovery strategies.
* Minimize downtime during security incidents or disasters.
* Ensure continuity of essential business operations.

🔐 Cybersecurity Policy

The cybersecurity policy covers:

* Password and account security
* Access control
* Data protection
* Device and network security
* Email and phishing awareness
* Software updates and patch management
* Employee security responsibilities

🚨 Incident Response Plan

The incident response plan defines the steps to follow during a cybersecurity incident:

1. Preparation
2. Identification
3. Containment
4. Eradication
5. Recovery
6. Post-Incident Review

🔄 Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

The plan focuses on keeping critical business operations running during disruptions and recovering systems and data after an incident.

It includes:

* Data backup strategy
* System recovery procedures
* Critical business operations
* Recovery priorities
* Emergency communication
* Disaster recovery procedures
* Business continuity measures

🏢 Target Organization

The project is designed for a small business with limited resources and focuses on practical, simple, and cost-effective cybersecurity and recovery measures.

📂 Project Contents

* Cybersecurity Policy
* Incident Response Plan
* Business Continuity Plan
* Disaster Recovery Plan
* Risk Assessment
* Backup & Recovery Strategy
* Security Guidelines

✅ Conclusion

This project provides a practical security and recovery framework for a small business. It helps the organization prevent cybersecurity incidents, respond effectively when incidents occur, recover critical systems and data, and maintain business continuity.
